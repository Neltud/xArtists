# Welcome to xArtists — Getting Started

**Art, AI intelligence and digital ownership — one connected experience.**

xArtists brings immersive art, AI-powered experiences and digital collectibles together in a MultiversX-connected dApp.

## Start in five steps

1. **Explore the ecosystem.** Visit the [xArtists dApp](https://neltud.github.io/xArtists/) and discover the art, museum and intelligence experiences.
2. **Connect your MultiversX wallet.** Use the wallet button to connect your own account. You remain in control of signing transactions.
3. **Discover Pulse, Yield and Sentinel.** Explore each AI pack and review its features and terms before purchasing.
4. **Experience the immersive museum and LIA intelligence.** Browse collections and informational paper/shadow signals. LIA does not execute autonomous live trades.
5. **Access your digital collectibles in My Packs.** Once issued and recognized on-chain, eligible NFTs can be displayed in your connected account.

## Packs, NFTs and memberships

Pulse, Yield and Sentinel are **one-time purchases**, not recurring subscriptions. Their associated NFT issuance depends on the verified payment and mainnet mint process; an automatic mint must not be assumed available until the production flow is validated.

**Whop TCA membership is a separate subscription for services**, not a Genesis NFT purchase.

## Safety and availability

A connected wallet does not authorize automatic transfers. Payments and NFT issuance have separate verification and operational safeguards. LIA signals are informational, not investment advice or trade instructions.

This guide describes the intended V3 user experience. Some functions may remain under controlled rollout; the application should display actual availability and signal freshness.
